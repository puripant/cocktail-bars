Cocktail Bars

IBA cocktails, each drawn as one bar. Switch between the current official list and the older Information is Beautiful sheet. Segment width is the ingredient's share of the pour, in centilitres. Dashes, garnishes and muddled solids have no volume, so they are listed after the bar.

The IBA list is taken from the recipe pages on iba-world.com, converted from millilitres. The other list is the IBA cocktail sheet compiled by Information is Beautiful, transcribed as listed, with unitless quantities read as centilitres. Where a recipe says "top with" or "splash", or measures in spoons or eggs, the amount is an estimate and the segment is hatched.
